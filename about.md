Ola Hungerford is a Principal Engineer supporting AI Enablement at Nordstrom, and a Maintainer, Working Group Lead, and Community Lead for the [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/). She spends much of her time on the question of how to make technology more useful, more interoperable, and more fun for humans.

Her technical background spans over two decades, from early web platforms to personalization systems, enterprise architecture, and modern AI/ML systems. Along the way she has played a lot of different roles, including (but not limited to) high school webmaster, software developer, IT support person, fitting room attendant [^1], project manager, database developer, Nesquik bunny mascot [^2], scoring software developer for professional bowling tournaments, and Director of IT.

Ola is also a vocalist, songwriter, and studio co-founder who explores how technology can augment human creativity. She has performed at venues and events including KEXP, Bumbershoot, and Decibel Festival, and continues to collaborate in the studio with her husband and creative partner, composer/producer/audio engineer Johnny Dexter Goss.

Sometimes she is also an ultrarunner, mostly because she loves any excuse to spend lots of time staring at nature and listening to things while snacking and moving slightly faster than hiking.

[^1]: Technically her first job at Nordstrom.

[^2]: Sometimes she still misses being the Nesquik bunny, even if it was only for a day at a Wal-Mart.
