I wrote a new post over on Substack, where I'll be posting going forward if you'd like to subscribe: [Humans are still the users](https://carbonbasedcontent.substack.com/p/humans-are-still-the-users).

<!--more-->

As AI platforms lean into more visual, interactive, human-friendly interfaces, it's worth remembering who we're ultimately designing solutions for (even while we're also designing for models). The post touches on privacy, security, token efficiency, new ways to stand out, and the fact that current protocols don't clearly specify whether a tool result is meant for the model or the user. (Plus, how you can contribute to the solution.)
