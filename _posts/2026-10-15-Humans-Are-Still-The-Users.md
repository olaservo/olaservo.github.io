I wrote a new post over on Substack: [Humans are still the users](https://carbonbasedcontent.substack.com/p/humans-are-still-the-users).

<!--more-->

As AI platforms lean into more visual, interactive, human-friendly interfaces, it's worth remembering who we're ultimately designing for - even while we're also designing for models. The post touches on privacy, security, token efficiency, new ways for brands to stand out, and the fact that current protocols don't specify whether a tool result is meant for the model or the user.
